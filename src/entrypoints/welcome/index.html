Welcome to PaperMemory

It is installed and already working. Four things to know; all of them can be changed later in Settings.

1What gets recorded

  • The papers you open On the sites listed below, PaperMemory saves the paper's title, authors, venue, year, links and BibTeX, when you opened it and how many times. Add your own tags, notes and code link. Other pages are ignored.
  • Stored in this browser Your Memory lives in this browser's extension storage. There is no PaperMemory account and no PaperMemory server.
  • Sync is optional, and goes to your own GitHub Off by default. If you turn it on with your GitHub token, your Memory is copied to a private gist in your GitHub account, and nowhere else.
  • What leaves your browser To fill in a paper's details or find where it was published, PaperMemory asks public services (arXiv, DBLP, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, CrossRef and OpenAlex) about that paper. Privacy policy
