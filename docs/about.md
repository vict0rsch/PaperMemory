# About PaperMemory

PaperMemory is free and open source software (MIT), developed by [vict0rsch](https://vict0rs.ch).

Share ideas or bugs in the [issues](https://github.com/vict0rsch/PaperMemory/issues).

## If you like PaperMemory

- Leave a [nice word](https://github.com/vict0rsch/PaperMemory/issues/81)
- Star the [project](https://github.com/vict0rsch/PaperMemory)
- Talk about PaperMemory on social media ([`#PaperMemory - @vict0rsch`](https://x.com/vict0rsch)) and in your lab or team
- Rate it on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/paper-memory/hmebhknlgddhfbbdhgplnillngljgmdi) (Chrome, Brave, Edge) or on [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/paper-memory/)
- [Buy me a coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/vict0rsch)

## If you want to help

I'm always looking for feedback, beta-testers and help with development (JavaScript, HTML, CSS). Reach out at `vsch [at] pm [dot] me` or in the [issues](https://github.com/vict0rsch/PaperMemory/issues): there are often small tasks that need help. Or contribute your own ideas in a pull request.
